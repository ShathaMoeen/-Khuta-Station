## Shared personalization and release rules

- Beginner: shorter sentences and familiar wording. Intermediate: moderate context. Advanced: source distinctions and limitations. The factual claims, evidence set and citations must remain the same across levels; do not imply additional facts at higher levels.
- Arabic/English adaptation must preserve meaning and references. A translated visitor answer remains tied to the Arabic passage IDs; cite the original source and clearly label any non-authoritative translation/adaptation.
- If retrieved evidence does not support a claim, omit it or abstain. Never allow model memory to fill gaps, invent dialogue, chronology, scenes, interpretation, or religious rulings.
- Ask responses should not produce an image. One image may appear only when the visitor first opens a station after image-rights review.
- Personal fatwa requests and individual worship/financial cases are referred to qualified human guidance. Do not infer sensitive characteristics from visitor behavior.
- TTS must speak exactly the final approved, citation-validated visitor text, without embellishment.
